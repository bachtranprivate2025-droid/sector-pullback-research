# Sector Pullback Research

Interactive historical research for SPY moving-average pullbacks and swing-low reversals, with equal-weight long-only US sector ETF baskets.

- Default sample: 2016-11-04 to 2026-11-03, Yahoo adjusted OHLC.
- Sector universe follows available history: 10 then 11 ETFs; XLC eligibility starts 2019-04-05 after its 200-session warmup.
- Original TradingView CSV sample remains available from the report selector.
- Sortable tables, 5/10/20-session views, trade details and downloadable research data.
- Static research snapshot; this site does not provide live prices or execute trades.

Historical results are research, not a guarantee of future returns. Methodology, costs and limitations are included in the report.
